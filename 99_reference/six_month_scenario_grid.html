Gaps and caveats

  • No open generator found for ship-traffic simulation, weather grids or infrastructure maps; use real open data.
  • Not verified in searches: Landsat access, WRF, SUMO, OSMnx, and the real-data feeds marked (unverified).
  • Check the license and maintenance status of every tool before use. SDV and Copulas use the Business Source License.
