# Canon decisions (2026-11-09)

The audits in `07_validation/reports/` (stream_alignment_audit, entity_graph_audit, geo_check)
found that the data streams, the later thread designs (T11-T22) and parts of the public record
tell a different story from the news corpus. These decisions settle every conflict. Where
sources disagree, **the base scenario (threads T1-T10) and the news corpus win**, and
everything else is changed to match.

## Clock

- D0 is the landfall day, Tuesday 2027-09-14 (local). Day offsets are local calendar days.
- Landfall: 01:00 CDT on D0 = 2027-09-14T06:00:00Z, on Mustang Island near Port Aransas, Category 3.
- Epoch of 2027-09-14T00:00:00Z is **1820880000**. (1821024000, used by older generators, is 40 hours late and must not be used.)
- Local time is US Central: CDT (UTC-5) until 2027-11-07 07:00Z, then CST (UTC-6).
- Scenario span: D-60 (2027-07-16) to D+119 (2028-01-11). Data streams cover at least D-30 (2027-08-15T00:00Z) to D+119 (2028-01-12T06:00Z).

## Story conflicts

1. **One oil spill.** About 9,000 barrels from MT Kalvora Star and MT Ostrel Bay at the Rendal Terminals berth in the Inner Harbor; cargo valves open 04:00Z to 05:30Z on D0. There is no "Auriga Meridian", no second spill at the anchorage and no "Pier 9" kinetic attack. Events, facts, stories and stream records that describe them are rewritten onto the canon spill or removed.
2. **Dark vessel.** The only dark vessel is the never-public Halcyon Drift (T2), with the FV Pelican Ridge identity spoof made public on D+38. Thread T12 observes that vessel and the two tankers; it adds no vessel of its own.
3. **Drones.** Two phases: pre-storm reconnaissance of utility and port sites D-10 to D-3 (public only from D+15), and night overflights of NAS Corpus Christi and the Army depot D+1 to D+10 (public from D+2). No sightings after D+11. The D+30 port loiter is removed. Covert drones never appear in ADS-B.
4. **Boil-water notices.** Citywide: D+4 07:00 local to D+39 09:00 local. Districts 3 and 4: English alert D+12 17:30 local; the Spanish notice reaches KNCB at 06:03 local on D+13; lifted D+42 11:00 local.
5. **Port and channel.** X-Ray D-3, Yankee D-2, Zulu D-1; closed from landfall; partial reopening D+30 (inner channel restricted for decontamination until D+44); closed again by the jetty blast at 01:00 local on D+44; second device found D+52; reopened D+58 (Nov 11). There is no phased reopening before D+30.
6. **911.** Peak about 2,900 calls on D+1; 2,109 is the D+2 rescue-call count.
7. **Federal aid.** Major disaster declaration D+4 (public assistance). Individual Assistance approved D+57, registration opens D+60. Anything at D0 is an information line only.
8. **Published times.** A story is never published before the fact it reports; if a fact has a time of day, same-day stories citing it are timed after it.

## Names

- The utility's public name is **Coastal Bend Power and Water Authority (CBPWA)**. "GridWater" is the scenario's internal code name and never appears in anything a participant can read (posts, alerts, logs). Asset tags keep the `GW-` prefix.
- Hospitals, terminals, contractors, vessels and broadcasters are fictional names placed on real sites. Real company and station names (refiners, hospital systems, newspapers, TV stations) do not appear in scenario data. The real names in `99_reference/geo/landmarks.geojson` are reference data only.
- One id per thing. Registries in `06_data_streams/` are the source of ids; streams and scenario files use those ids.

## Geography

- Ground truth is `99_reference/geo/` (USACE channels, NOAA land areas and anchorages, Census roads, real landmark points), used through `scripts/geo.py`.
- `python scripts/check_geo.py` must report zero violations: vessels in water and, if deep-draft, in the dredged channels, anchorages or open Gulf; fixed assets on land (or in water for sondes and buoys); incident locations on streets; vehicles on roads; aircraft on the ground only at airfields and helipads.
- Logs are generated first; the map viewer is built from the logs, never the other way round.

## Data hygiene

- No answer-revealing columns or labels in delivered streams (for example `AttackSig`, `phase_label`, notes that explain the attack).
- Never-public terms (see `02_public_record/restricted_terms.json`) do not appear in any public-voice stream (news, social posts, alerts, press statements, dispatch narratives); date-gated terms appear there only on or after their public day. Raw technical data that a team is meant to investigate (AIS static fields, host names in logs, account ids in payments) carries the real identifiers, with no explanation attached.
